Claude Design + the new Claude models = marketing at scale.

Go to your codebase, open Claude Code and run:

```bash
/design-sync
```

Or simply ask Claude Code to `extract your design system.` Then open the Claude desktop app, go to the Design tab, click **Design** and select your synced design system.

Now you can create landing pages, product visuals and marketing assets in your own style.
